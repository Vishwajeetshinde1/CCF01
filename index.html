Follow a bale from farm to fabric

Every step is recorded, so a bale can always be traced back to the farmers who grew it.

  1. Farmers grow the cottonNuziveedu seed is planted. Each farmer's village, district, state and variety are on record.
  2. Seed cotton reaches CCFPurchases from many farmers are grouped into a numbered lot.
  3. Ginning and balingLint is ginned in-house and pressed into bales.
  4. Fibre is testedHVI results such as micronaire and staple length are saved with the lot.
  5. QR on the baleScanning it opens the lot page with the farmer details.
  6. Cloth at NSL TextilesThe traced cotton becomes finished fabric.
